# Hilton Cost Concept Map V1

Disclosed transaction price, GO price, placement price, rights price, subscription price and market price are separate observations. Source-described “成本” is a SOURCE_CLAIM and requires source context. Derived adjusted/composite cost needs formula, inputs, date and bounds. “莊家成本” is NOT_PUBLICLY_OBSERVABLE unless directly disclosed; it must never be stored as VERIFIED_FACT.
